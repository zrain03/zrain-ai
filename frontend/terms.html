1. Purpose

Zrain AI is a free, experimental AI assistant developed by Zrain to help people learn, create, code and explore ideas. The service may change or be temporarily unavailable while under development.

2. AI limitations

AI-generated answers can be mistaken, incomplete or outdated. Web research is limited to Wikipedia and explicitly approved public websites; it is not a general-purpose search engine. Website reading may fail, and sources can be incomplete or outdated. The service does not guarantee factual accuracy or provide professional advice. Verify important information using reliable sources and relevant qualified professionals.

3. Free capacity and fair use

To keep the service at zero monetary cost, we enforce a personal daily allowance and a shared capacity limit. A personal allowance is a ceiling, not a guarantee that shared resources remain available. Automated abuse, evasion of rate limits and attempts to overwhelm the service are prohibited.

4. Attachments

Only supported document and image formats may be attached. Browser processing may truncate long document text, and scanned or encrypted PDFs may not work. Image understanding uses a separate AI model and may not always be available. Do not submit content you do not have the right to share or sensitive personal information.

5. Local chat history and personal data

Live Web Preview supports static HTML, CSS and browser JavaScript inside an isolated iframe. It does not run backend servers, Python, React builds, or localhost services, and network access is blocked. Generated code should be reviewed before use outside the preview.

Conversation history is stored on your device and can be deleted or exported. It is not synchronized across devices. The AI service receives messages and selected attachment contents needed to answer your questions. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

6. Contact

Questions or feedback: zrain.dev@gmail.com.
